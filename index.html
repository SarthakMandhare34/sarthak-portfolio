Problem & Practical Solution

Scattered clinic directories and fragmented medical documentation often delay prompt patient care. LifeLink addresses this by integrating 19 Mumbai suburban railway hubs into a localized specialist directory, pairing it with rapid AI symptom evaluation and a seamless clinical appointment workflow.

Technical Architecture & Key Deliverables
  • Engineered an end-to-end healthcare prototype featuring AI triage, geospatial specialist discovery, appointment scheduling, tamper-evident digital prescriptions, and an Emergency Health Passport.
